Who We Are

Kogi State Senior Secondary Education Board

Our Mandate

KGSSEB is established to provide strategic leadership, plan, coordinate, and monitor senior secondary education. We formulate policies, oversee administration, and collaborate with stakeholders to enhance service delivery.

Our Vision

To build a vibrant, inclusive, and high-performing senior secondary education system that nurtures critical thinkers, innovators, and responsible citizens.

Our Mission

To provide quality, well-supervised, and adequately resourced senior secondary education that equips students with the knowledge, skills, and values required for success in further education and society.

Our Departments & Functions

A detailed look at the functional units powering our education system.

Schools & Colleges

  • Supervision of all Senior Secondary Schools.
  • Approval/monitoring of new schools.
  • Posting of principals and vice principals.
  • Inspection of facilities and records.
  • Liaison with Zonal Offices.

Human Resources

  • Recruitment, appointment, and posting of staff.
  • Maintain personnel records/service docs.
  • Staff promotion and discipline.
  • Training and professional development.
  • Ensuring staff welfare and ethics.

ICT Department

  • Maintenance of hardware and software.
  • Support for e-learning/digital literacy.
  • Database and EMIS management.
  • Network security and systems backup.
  • Management of website/email services.

Quality Assurance

  • Conducting regular school inspections.
  • Monitoring curriculum implementation.
  • Assessment of learning outcomes.
  • Monitoring teacher performance.
  • Ensuring compliance with benchmarks.

Planning & Research

  • Strategic and operational planning.
  • Research and needs assessments.
  • Maintaining EMIS.
  • Analysis of performance metrics.
  • Producing statistical reports.

Finance & Accounts

  • Preparation of annual budget.
  • Processing salaries and allowances.
  • Bank reconciliations and records.
  • Payment processing for projects.
  • Financial reporting and audit.
